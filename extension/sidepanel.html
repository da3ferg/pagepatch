PagePatch

Your key stays in this browser's session storage until you clear it or close Chrome. Each request forwards it to the provider through the PagePatch API, which doesn't store or log it.

Before you send

To suggest changes, PagePatch sends the following to your chosen provider through the PagePatch API:

  • a simplified copy of the section you selected (tags, classes, visible text, link paths) and its parent's layout
  • your messages in this conversation
  • when you generate a source patch: a few matching files from the repository and branch you choose

It never sends form values, cookies, page storage, URL query strings, or anything outside your selection.

Click the PagePatch toolbar button on a page to select a section.
